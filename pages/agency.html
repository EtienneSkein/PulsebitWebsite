Engagement Models

Four ways to work with the agency.

01

Campaign sprint

One defined push with a start date and an end date. Strategy, creative and media built around a single moment.

  • Messaging and creative concept
  • Assets across the channels that matter
  • Performance read at the end

Best when you have a launch, a season or an event to land.

02

Always-on retainer

A standing monthly rhythm of strategy, content and paid media, with reporting you can take into a board meeting.

  • Monthly content and campaign calendar
  • Paid media managed and optimised
  • Fixed reporting cadence

Best when growth needs to compound rather than spike.

03

Brand and creative project

Identity, messaging, landing experience and launch assets, delivered as a project with a clear finish line.

  • Positioning and messaging platform
  • Identity and campaign art direction
  • Landing pages and launch assets

Best when the story is the bottleneck, not the budget.

04

Marketing audit

A review of your funnel, tracking, creative and spend, delivered as a written findings report with a ranked fix list.

  • Where the funnel is leaking
  • Whether tracking is telling the truth
  • What to change first, in order

Best when the spend is going out and you cannot see what it bought.

Delivery Sequence

How a campaign actually runs.

  1. 01

    Positioning and audience

    Who we are actually talking to, what they already believe about your category and what the offer really is. Most campaigns that fail were aimed at everyone.

    Output: messaging platform
  2. 02

    Channel and funnel plan

    Which platforms, what each one is there to do, and how someone moves from first seeing you to making contact. A channel without a job attached is just spend.

    Output: channel and funnel plan
  3. 03

    Creative production

    Copy, design, video cuts and landing pages, built to be tested rather than admired. Several angles go out, not one perfect execution.

    Output: campaign assets
  4. 04

    Launch and measure

    Tracking is set up before any money is spent, and what counts as a conversion is agreed before the first ad runs. Otherwise you are guessing in retrospect.

    Output: live campaign with tracking
  5. 05

    Read, cut and scale

    We stop what is not working, put budget behind what is, and report it in terms of enquiries and revenue rather than impressions.

    Output: monthly performance read
